How it works

It takes 30 seconds to learn and a lifetime to hit 100%.

Hold to pour

A spout sweeps across the top on its own. Hold anywhere on the screen to pour, release to stop. That is the whole control scheme.

Fill to the line

Each chamber has a dashed fill line. Too little or too much costs points, and so does sand that spills. Tap Settle when you are happy.

Get one number

Your accuracy, to one decimal — like 97.4%. Three tries a day, your best counts. Then the jar goes on your shelf until tomorrow.

Small game, lots of feel

Made for a calm minute with your morning coffee — and a quiet rivalry with your friends.

The same puzzle for everyone

A new jar every day at midnight, identical worldwide. Compare scores without spoilers.

A week that builds

Monday is one gentle chamber. By Saturday it is four chambers and ledges that bounce your sand.

A shelf of sand art

Every day's best pour is kept as a layered jar. Pour all week and unlock rare sand colours.

Ghost pours

Share your pour as a link. Friends see your timing as a blue stream and get one bonus try to beat you.

Share a replay clip

One tap turns your best pour into a short video, ready for your story or group chat.

Sound and touch

Soft sand hiss, haptic ticks and a slow score reveal. Plays fully offline.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your scores, streak and jars are stored on your phone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Puzzles are generated on your device from the date, so you can pour on a plane. Ads and purchases need a connection.

When does the new puzzle arrive?

At midnight in your local time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle for the same date.

How do ghost pours work?

After a pour, tap Share. Your friend opens the link, sees your score, and gets one bonus try with your pour timing shown as a blue stream.

Are there ads?

There are no ads while you pour. A small banner appears on menu screens, and an occasional full-screen ad may show between results. Pour Pass removes all ads.

I bought Pour Pass on another phone

Install One Pour with the same Google account and open the app. Your purchase is restored automatically by Google Play.
