Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to the One Pour app for Android (package com.seus.onepour) and this website.

One Pour is a daily puzzle game. We built it to work without accounts and without our own servers. Your game data stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sells an optional upgrade through Google Play; those Google services process some data as described below.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the developer of One Pour. Name and postal address are listed in the Impressum. Contact: ahseust@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, because we do not run any server for the app:

  • your tries, scores and the timing of your pours for each daily puzzle;
  • images of your saved jars, your streak and unlocked sand colours;
  • practice counters, the date you first played (to pace ads) and whether you own Pour Pass.

You can delete all of it at any time: Android Settings › Apps › One Pour › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Sharing your pour

When you tap Share or Share clip, the app creates a link or a short video and hands it to the Android share menu. The link contains only the puzzle date, your score and the timing of your pour. It contains no name, account or device identifier. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and UK; Google LLC elsewhere). To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic data.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later in the app's consent settings, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, or opt out of personalised ads. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised ads and the related device access; our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)) for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and frequency capping.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Pour Pass is sold through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see them. The app only receives confirmation of whether you own Pour Pass, so it can unlock the features and restore them on a new phone.

We do not sell your data, do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

One Pour is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in some EU countries) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app with personalised ads, contact us and we will help remove any data we can influence.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because game data is only on your device, you control it directly as described above. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your country of residence.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. The hosting provider may log your IP address and browser details briefly for security and to deliver the pages.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date at the top.
